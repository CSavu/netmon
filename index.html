    The charts' numbers as a table

    Problem minute: at least 1 router ping lost, or at least 2 internet pings lost, or at least 3 router replies of 150 ms or more. These thresholds are this monitor's own, not an industry standard. With lost pings leaves out the minutes where the router was only slow.

    Not counted: stretches under 3 minutes, the first and last minute of each stretch (including the minute running now), minutes when the Asus lost a ping, and marked router restarts. Wi-Fi stays separate from cable, because lost pings on Wi-Fi can come from the radio link.

    Measuring: one ping about every 0,55 s per address (no reply in 1 s = lost), one HTTPS connection test and one DNS lookup about every second (2 s limit), Wi-Fi signal every 5 s. All from this laptop, only while it's awake.
